Turn scary information into structured understanding.

DataTherapy takes a headline, a rumor, or a spiral and returns a calm brief: how serious it looks, how personal it is, how urgent it is, how certain it is, what is likely versus myth, and what a grounded next step might be. It is not therapy, not medical advice, and not emergency support.

If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services or local crisis resources. DataTherapy does not provide medical, legal, or financial advice.

Four anchors

  • Seriousness — how severe the issue appears from the language, not from the panic around it.
  • Relevance — how directly it seems to touch the person reading.
  • Urgency — whether something must happen now, or whether visibility is being mistaken for immediacy.
  • Certainty — how confirmed or speculative the input looks, including rumor language.
